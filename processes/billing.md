# Billing

**Owner:** Hana Kim (Ops lead).

## How customers are billed

- Through **Stripe**, invoiced **monthly per venue**.
- Billing is per venue; contracts are per account (`glossary.md`).
- List price and discounts: `company/pricing.md`.

## Failed payments

Chased by hand. Hana Kim checks Stripe **every Monday**.

## Not in this repo

Invoices, payment status and anything else about a specific customer stay in Stripe (team rule 3, `company/team-rules.md`).

# Hiring and new-hire setup

**There is no hiring process.** This doc records what happens today, nothing more.

## What happens today

1. Mara Voss sources candidates.
2. Two people interview. Which two is not fixed.
3. Mara Voss decides.

The Head of People role has been open since March (`team/who-owns-what.md`).

## New-hire accounts

- The Notion onboarding checklist says a new hire gets all accounts on day one.
- In practice most people wait until week two.

When someone joins, whoever has time that week walks them through things. Nobody owns this (`open-questions.md`). For a new colleague, start with `README.md` in this repo.

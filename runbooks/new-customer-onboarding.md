# Runbook: onboard a new customer

**Owner:** Lukas Weber.
**Use when:** a contract is signed in HubSpot.
**Goal:** the customer publishes their first schedule as fast as possible. That moment ends **T2L** (`glossary.md`). Typical today: 1 to 3 days. The pitch promises under a day (`company/overview.md`).

## Steps

1. Confirm the contract is signed in HubSpot. T2L starts here.
2. Book a 45-minute setup call with the customer.
3. Import the venue and staff list from the customer's CSV with the importer.
   - If the file has names with non-Latin characters, or was exported from Apple Numbers, the importer fails. Ask Noor Haddad for the workaround. The workaround is **not in the brain** yet (`open-questions.md`).
4. Configure pay rules for the customer's country (`glossary.md`).
5. The customer publishes their first schedule. T2L ends here.
6. Hold the 7-day check-in call.
7. Hand over to support (Jonas Berg, `processes/support-escalation.md`).

## Do not

- Do not save the customer's CSV, staff list or any export in a doc, spreadsheet, AI tool or this repo (team rule 3, `company/team-rules.md`).
- Do not mix up account and venue: contracts are per account, billing is per venue (`glossary.md`).

# Fernway at a glance

**Fernway** is a remote-first B2B SaaS company, founded in 2024 and registered in Berlin. The team works from five countries. English is the working language (`company/team-rules.md`).

## Product

**Fernway Shifts** does shift planning and labour-cost forecasting for independent cafe and restaurant groups with 3 to 20 locations.

- Managers build schedules in it.
- It checks schedules against local break and overtime rules (see "pay rules" in `glossary.md`).
- It projects the wage bill before the week is published.

## Customers

- About 40 accounts and about 140 paying venues. One account can have many venues (`glossary.md`).
- Markets: Germany, the Netherlands, Ireland.

## Stage

- Seed round raised in early 2025.
- 15 people plus contractors (`team/who-owns-what.md`).

## Why customers choose Fernway

Competitors are built for enterprise chains and take weeks to configure. Fernway's pitch is that a venue is live in under a day. Onboarding speed is the whole positioning, which is why **T2L** (time to live) is the number the team watches (`glossary.md`, `processes/onboarding.md`).

Note: the pitch says "under a day", while the onboarding process records a typical T2L of 1 to 3 days (`processes/onboarding.md`). Both figures are from the source; nobody has reconciled them.

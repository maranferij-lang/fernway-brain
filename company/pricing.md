# Pricing rules

## List price

1. **EUR 39 per venue per month.** Billing is per venue, contracts are per account (`glossary.md`).
2. **15% discount for annual prepay.**
3. **Anything below that is a CEO decision.** Only Mara Voss can approve it.

## Pricing exceptions ("Mara-check")

Any price below the list price or the annual-prepay discount is a pricing exception. The team calls it a **Mara-check** (`glossary.md`). Every pricing exception goes through Mara Voss.

This is a known bottleneck (pack §2). This doc does not change the rule. It only records it.

## Who to ask

| Question | Person |
|---|---|
| Can we go below EUR 39 per venue or beyond the 15% prepay discount? | Mara Voss (CEO) |
| How is a venue invoiced? | Hana Kim, see `processes/billing.md` |

Anything about specific accounts' prices is kept out of this repo on purpose (`CONTRIBUTING.md`, "Excluded on purpose").
